Questions, answered honestly

Is NAVORA free?

Browsing and recommendations are free. Some listed courses are paid by their providers; we always show a free alternative where one exists.

Do you host the courses?

No. We link to original sources and credit them. We never copy course material.

Are the ratings real?

We only show ratings from a named external source. Where we do not have one, we say "Rating unavailable". Student reviews on NAVORA are shown separately.

Is this AI?

The recommender is a transparent scoring model, not a black box. The study mentor works offline with rule-based answers; an external LLM can be connected optionally.

How do you make money?

Possible future sources are affiliate links, clearly labelled sponsorships and premium features. None changes how recommendations are ranked. See the disclosure.
