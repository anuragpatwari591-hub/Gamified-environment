Privacy Policy

NAVORA is an early-stage student project. This policy describes what the software actually does. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional before any commercial launch.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, and a salted password hash (we never store your password).
  • Learning DNA: stream, year, interests, goals, level, study time, language, budget and format preferences, which you choose.
  • Activity: resources started/completed, study minutes you log, quiz attempts, saved items, feedback and reviews.
  • Usage analytics: searches and outbound resource clicks (including whether a click used an affiliate link).

What we do not collect

No phone number, address, government ID, payment details or precise location.

How we use it

To personalise recommendations, show your progress, and improve the catalogue. We do not sell personal data.

Third parties

When you open an external resource you leave NAVORA and that site's own policy applies. If an optional external AI service is enabled by the operator, mentor messages may be sent to it; by default none is.

Your choices

You can edit your Learning DNA at any time. To delete your account and data, contact the site operator.

Cookies

One essential, HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in and protects forms (CSRF). There are no advertising cookies.
