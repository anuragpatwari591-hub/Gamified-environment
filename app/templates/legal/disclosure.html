Affiliate & sponsored content disclosure

Affiliate links

Some resource links can be affiliate links. If you buy through one, NAVORA may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. A resource with an affiliate link shows a notice on its page. Whether a resource has an affiliate link plays no part in how it is ranked or recommended.

Sponsored content

Sponsored placements are paid for by their sponsors, appear in clearly marked boxes labelled Sponsored, and are never presented as organic recommendations or counted in your match scores.

Current status

At the time of writing NAVORA has no active affiliate partnerships and no paying sponsors. The infrastructure to track clicks and label placements exists; any sponsored item currently shown is marked Demo Data and is only an example of the format.

Free first

Free options are prioritised for students who choose Free-first mode, and paid courses are shown with free alternatives wherever one exists.
